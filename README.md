# Nazo's Love Story

Create an incredibly cute, romantic, emotional, and visually beautiful birthday website dedicated to my girl, Nazo.

The website should feel like it was lovingly made specifically for her—not like a generic birthday template. The overall experience should be dreamy, soft, intimate, playful, elegant, and memorable. Think of a little digital love letter combined with a magical birthday experience.

Overall Design

Use a soft romantic aesthetic with:

Warm blush pink, soft lavender, cream, white, and subtle rose tones

Soft gradients and gentle glowing effects

Tiny floating hearts, stars, sparkles, flowers, and subtle particles

Rounded cards and elegant typography

Smooth scrolling and beautiful micro-animations

Subtle glassmorphism where appropriate, but DON'T overdo it

Lots of breathing room so it feels premium rather than cluttered

A warm, dreamy atmosphere rather than a dark or overly flashy design

The website should look cute enough to make her smile but sophisticated enough to feel genuinely special.

Avoid generic corporate UI, excessive neon colors, cheesy stock illustrations, excessive emojis, or anything that looks obviously AI-generated.

1. Opening Screen — The Surprise

Start with a beautiful full-screen opening scene.

Display:

"Hey Nazo..."

Then underneath:

"I made a little something for you."

Have a glowing button:

"Open Your Surprise ♡"

When she clicks it, trigger a beautiful transition with floating hearts/confetti and reveal:

"Happy Birthday, Nazo! ♡"

Add a subtle animated background with tiny stars and floating hearts.

Make this first interaction feel magical.

2. Main Hero Section

After opening:

Happy Birthday, Nazo ♡

Subtitle:

"To my favorite person, on her special day."

Include a beautiful animated birthday cake with candles.

When the user clicks/blows out the candles, animate the candles going out and reveal a small message:

"One wish for you: may this year be even more beautiful than you are."

Add a small scroll indicator saying:

"There's more... ↓"

3. The Birthday Letter

Create an elegant handwritten-style letter section.

Heading:

"A little something I wanted to tell you..."

Write the section so it feels personal and sincere, with placeholders where I can insert my own words.

Example structure:

"Nazo,

I don't think a website could ever properly explain how special you are to me, but I wanted to try anyway...

Meri Pyari Januuu

Thank you for being you.
Thank you for all the little moments.
And most importantly, thank you for being someone I can call mine.

Happy Birthday, my girl. ♡"

Make the letter appear gradually as the user scrolls, almost like it is being written.

4. Memory Gallery

Create a gorgeous photo section titled:

"Little moments, big memories ♡"

Add a responsive gallery where I can easily replace placeholder images with our photos.

Don't make it a boring rectangular grid.

Use a beautiful editorial-style layout with different image sizes, rounded corners, subtle rotations, and hover animations.

When an image is clicked:

Open it in a beautiful fullscreen lightbox

Add a caption underneath

Smooth transition

Allow closing easily

Include placeholder captions such as:

"One of my favorite memories."

"Look at you 🥹"

"I'd choose this moment again."

Make it easy for me to replace both images and captions in the code.

5. "Things I Love About You"

Create a cute interactive section.

Heading:

"Things I love about Nazo ♡"

Instead of simply listing them, create small floating cards.

Examples:

Your smile

The way you laugh

Your little habits

How you make ordinary days better

The way you care

Simply... you

When each card is clicked, it expands with a short personalized message.

Make the cards gently float and react to the mouse/touch.

7. Interactive Gift

Create a section with a beautifully wrapped animated gift box.

Text:

"I have one more thing for you..."

Button:

"Open it ♡"

"You."

Make this playful and cute.

8. Birthday Wish

Create a dreamy section with a night-sky-inspired background.

Heading:

"Make a wish, Nazo ✨"

Add a button:

"Make a Wish ♡"

When clicked:

Display:

The final section should be emotionally beautiful.

Display:

Then:

"If I could give you one thing this year,

Have subtle hearts floating upward in the background.

Music

Include an optional music player.

Do NOT autoplay aggressively.

Create a cute small music button in the corner:

♫ Our song

Clicking it should play/pause a local audio file that I can replace easily.

Include a clearly marked placeholder:

Animation Style

Use animations that feel soft and romantic, not excessive.

Include:

Fade-ins

Gentle slide-ups

Floating hearts

Soft particle effects

Image hover animations

Smooth section transitions

Button micro-interactions

Gift opening animation

Cake candle interaction

Shooting star animation

Scroll-triggered animations

Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

Important Design Direction

This is extremely important:

Do not make this look like a generic "Happy Birthday HTML Template."

It should feel like a personal digital love story.

Imagine Nazo opening the link on her phone at night and slowly scrolling through it. Every section should make her feel like:

"He actually made all of this for me."

The website should progressively become more emotional as she scrolls.

Start playful and cute → become personal → become romantic → finish emotionally.

Use elegant typography and beautiful spacing.

Make the final result feel like something that could genuinely make her smile, blush, and maybe cry a little.

Most importantly:

Make Nazo feel like the entire website exists because she is the main character. ♡
